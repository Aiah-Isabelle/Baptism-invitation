Godparents’ Attire

Our dear Godparents, we kindly ask you to wear white, church-appropriate attire for the baptism. Please choose something neat, modest and comfortable, such as a white dress, blouse, polo or barong. Your presence and loving guidance will make Aiah’s special day even more meaningful.

Guests

Our dear guests are welcome to wear any color of their choice in semi-formal or casual attire. Most importantly, come ready to share in the joy and love of this memorable day!

RSVP

We would love to celebrate this special day with you! Kindly confirm your attendance by replying to this invitation before October 25. We look forward to sharing this blessed occasion and creating beautiful memories together.

Gift guide

Your presence, love and prayers are already the most precious gift we could ask for. If you wish to bless our little one with something special, any thoughtful present or cash will be truly appreciated and received with grateful hearts.

  • Clothes (1-3 years old)
  • Cash/money
  • Educational toys
  • Unilove products (shampoo or wipes)

No matter how big or small, every gift given with love will be cherished. Thank you for being part of our little one's special day!

Safety reminder

To keep our little one safe and comfortable during this special celebration, we kindly ask everyone to follow these simple reminders:

  • Please avoid kissing the baby
  • No smoking, please
  • Sanitize your hands before touching the baby
  • Please wear a face mask if you have a cold or are not feeling well

Thank you for helping us keep our little one safe, healthy and surrounded by love!
